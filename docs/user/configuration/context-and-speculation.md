| Flag | Env | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--model-max-context <tokens>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_MAX_CONTEXT` | `40960` | Max per-sequence context (prompt + generation). The KV pool must be able to hold one of them or the load is refused. On the 27B the attention bounds it too: at most 524,288 on `bf16`, 1,048,576 on `hq-e8-2b`; past that the start is refused. Flash-Next has no such bound. |
| `--model-default-max-tokens <n\|0>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS` | `38912` | The `max_tokens` of a request that sends none (see [Chat completions](../api/chat-completions.md)), its reasoning included, on both models. Never more than what the prompt leaves of `--model-max-context`, so a long prompt is never refused for it. An explicit cap always wins. `0` = none: such a request may generate to the end of the context, as before. (`make` knob `DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS`.) |
| `--model-prefill-chunk <tokens>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_PREFILL_CHUNK` | `1024`; `2048` for the 27B under the built-in profiles | The prefill chunk width, a nonzero multiple of 128. The program's prefill scratch is reserved for it at load. |
| `--spec-decode-lanes <n>` | `IGNIS_SPEC_DECODE_LANES` | `3` | Flash-Next only, `1..=8`: the sequences decoded at once. The lanes share the KV pool ([below](vram-and-kv-pool.md#the-kv-pool)): min(524,288 tokens, lanes × `--model-max-context`), never below one `--model-max-context` sequence and a page per retained slot. At `make`'s 262,144 tokens that is one context at one lane and 524,288 tokens from two lanes on, so one lane leaves the expert cache about 1 GiB more, and further lanes cost only their state; at 131,072 tokens three lanes get 393,216. The 27B serves a fixed 8 lanes and refuses the flag. `make MODEL=flash-next` runs 3 lanes at up to 262,144 tokens each (the checkpoint's trained positions); the `make` knob is `LANES`. |
| `--model-decode-share <percent>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_DECODE_SHARE` | `25` on both models | The part of the time decoding lanes keep while a prompt prefills, 0-99: after a chunk that took `t`, the next chunk of the same prompt waits `t * s / (1 - s)` of wall time while they decode, and nothing waits when no lane decodes or after the prompt's last chunk. It trades the prefilling request's TTFT (about x1.33 at 25, x2 at 50, only while lanes decode) for the lanes' rate (15.6-18.3 tok/s on Flash-Next instead of ~1; why 25: [ADR 0018](../../adr/0018-chunk-level-prefill-decode-interleaving.md)). `--model-decode-share 0` restores one decode round per chunk, the pacing before [#92](../../findings/2026-10-07-27b-prefill-chunk-width.md); 50 splits time evenly. A prompt alone is unaffected. |
| `--model-kv-format <fmt>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_KV_FORMAT` | `hq-e8-2b` | `hq-e8-2b` (serving) or `bf16` (retained; the format every correctness oracle runs against — [ADR 0022](../../adr/0022-two-kv-formats-bf16-as-oracle.md)). Decides what a pool byte budget is worth in tokens. |
| `--model-prefill-attention <precision>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_PREFILL_ATTENTION` | `fp8` | The 27B only: the precision of the prompt attention's band pass over an `hq-e8-2b` prefill chunk. `fp8` runs it in 8 bits (Q·Kᵀ in INT8, P·V in E4M3); `fp16` is the FP16-accumulate fallback. Fixed for the life of the load. Flash-Next's attention is QSA, with no such pass: a Flash-Next start refuses an explicit value. |
| `--model-exception-weights <format>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_EXCEPTION_WEIGHTS` | `bf16` | The 27B only: how its nine BF16 exception tensors (the attention projections of the early GQA layers, the GDN output of layer 4) are held on the GPU. `bf16` keeps them as the artifact stores them. `fp8` converts them at load to FP8 row-scale: 0.53 GB less VRAM, cold prefill ~3% faster at 14K-105K, the same 4K KLD, but two decision fixtures read differently ([finding](../../findings/2026-10-21-the-bf16-exception-tensors-in-fp8.md)); they are multiplied on the FP8 tensor cores in prefill and in any call past 8 rows (a verify round of two lanes or more), with BF16 activations in a decode round and a one-lane verify round. Fixed for the life of the load. Flash-Next has none: a Flash-Next start refuses an explicit value. |
| `--vram-kv-pool-bytes <bytes\|tokens>` | `IGNIS_VRAM_KV_POOL_BYTES` | the KV pool policy's | The KV pool, on both models: a byte count (`K`/`M`/`G` suffixes) or a token count, `<n>tok`, `<n>Ktok` or `<n>Mtok` with binary multipliers (`512Ktok` is 524,288 tokens: the same context on either model and either format, where a byte count is worth a different one on each). It replaces the policy's size ([below](vram-and-kv-pool.md#the-kv-pool)). Smaller than one `--model-max-context` sequence plus a page per retained slot, or past the VRAM budget, fails the load by name. (`make` knob `KV_POOL_BYTES`.) |
| `--reuse-kv-host-pool-bytes <bytes>` | `IGNIS_REUSE_KV_HOST_POOL_BYTES` | `2G` | The KV-RAM host tier. Page-locked whole at start and held for the life of the load, so it is RAM the process holds even idle — and the figure Windows reports as shared GPU memory. `0` disables the host tier. (`make` sets `8G`, and Flash-Next's own `2G` as `--qwen38flashnext-reuse-kv-host-pool-bytes`: knob `FN_KV_HOST_POOL_BYTES`.) |
| `--spec-backend <backend>` | `IGNIS_SPEC_BACKEND` | unset | Speculative decoding backend: `dflash2` (Qwen3.8-27B), `mtp` (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, see [below](flash-next-mtp.md)), `off`, or unset for none. |
| `--spec-draft-tokens <n>` | `IGNIS_SPEC_DRAFT_TOKENS` | — | 1..7. Required with `--spec-backend dflash2`; with `--spec-backend mtp` the most drafts a lane verifies per round (default 2). |
| `--spec-draft-rows <n>` | `IGNIS_SPEC_DRAFT_ROWS` | `0` (= 8) | Flash-Next MTP only: the rows one verify round may take across all lanes, `0` or 2..32. Each lane drafts `min(draft tokens, rows / lanes - 1)`, so `3` drafts at one lane only, and past 8 five to eight lanes draft too (`16`: 3 drafts up to four lanes, 2 at five, 1 from six to eight). A budget past 8 reserves the wider rounds' VRAM, taken from the expert cache: about 50 MB at 16 rows. Past 8 the round is held numerically equivalent to speculation off, not token for token: greedy output may differ from it where two tokens are nearly tied ([ADR 0056](../../adr/0056-speculation-past-eight-verify-rows-is-numerically-equivalent.md)). Measured slower than 8 on both the RTX 5090 and the RTX PRO 6000 ([below](flash-next-mtp.md)). (`make` knob `DRAFT_ROWS`.) |
| `--spec-draft-head <head>` | `IGNIS_SPEC_DRAFT_HEAD` | `full` | Needs `--spec-backend`. The head the drafter proposes with: `full` (the target's output head) or `shortlist` (the artifact's Q4 head over the 131,072 most frequent tokens, +356 MB of VRAM). What a round accepts is still the target's choice, so the text changes only at near ties; measured on coding prompts `shortlist` accepts 7% fewer tokens per round and is slower overall ([finding](../../findings/2026-09-24-upstream-quick-wins-ab.md)). (`make` knob `DRAFT_HEAD`.) |
| `--model-rope-scaling <spec>` | `IGNIS_MODEL_ROPE_SCALING` | `auto` | `auto` decides from the model's `--model-max-context`: no scaling up to the checkpoint's trained 262,144 positions, else YaRN at `max_context / 262,144` (524,288 is 2, 786,432 is 3, 1,048,576 is 4); past 64x (16,777,216 tokens) the start is refused. `none` is the linear table whatever the context; `yarn:F[,t=<c>][,bf=<n>][,bs=<n>]` rescales the envelope by `F` in (1, 64]. An explicit value overrides `auto`. Both models apply it; on Flash-Next one table serves attention, its sparse-attention indexer and the MTP head, with YaRN's attention factor on queries and keys alike, as the model's reference implementations apply it ([ADR 0059](../../adr/0059-flash-next-applies-yarn-on-q-and-k.md)). The load log (`ignis.model.loaded`) names the table run (`rope_scaling`) beside the setting (`rope_scaling_setting`). (`make` knob `ROPE_SCALING`.) |
